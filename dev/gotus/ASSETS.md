# Gotus assets

The word bank is copied from `dev/motus/data/words.json` (11,780 targets and
17,991 accepted six-letter words). Presenter and studio images come from the
local Motus prototype. The five original MP3 files are served in `/sound/gotus/`.

The following transparent PNGs were generated on 2026-10-23 with the built-in
image generation tool. WebP trophy variants are resized exports of the PNG.

## Logo — `/gotus/gotus-logo.png`

Production uses the 900-pixel WebP export `/gotus/gotus-logo.webp` (about 41 KB).

Reference: `.tmp/motus/pour recup logo.jpg`.

Prompt: Create a finished transparent PNG logo spelling GOTUS, G O T U S. Use
the reference CD-ROM cover's top MOTUS lettering only: five equal mauve-purple
discs in a horizontal row, white tall thin rounded geometric uppercase letters,
the same condensed type and subtle beveled shaded edges with perimeter shadows.
No cover, presenter, background, orange swoosh or tagline. Generous transparent
margins; readable as both a tiny player badge and a heading.
